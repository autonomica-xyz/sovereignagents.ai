## What it is

x402 revives the HTTP `402 Payment Required` status for stablecoins. A server states its price, the client signs a payment, and the request goes through. On 14 July 2026 the Linux Foundation announced the operational launch of the x402 Foundation, with 40 founding members, to govern x402 as an open, vendor-neutral standard.[^lf]

## Why it matters to an organism

Most of the agent economy that pays at all pays this way. Conway Automaton, for example, buys its compute from Conway Cloud in USDC over x402.[^conway] An organism selling services will meet most of its customers here.

## Volume, as measured by third parties

Independent trackers with published methods, read on 26–27 November 2026, put roughly $1.4–1.6 million of filtered volume through x402 over the prior 30 days, against about $1.7–1.8 million unfiltered. x402stats.io reported $1,446,257 organic versus $1,806,552 raw, and 91 sellers that met its organic bar. Barker, measuring Base via the Coinbase CDP API and last updated 26 November 2026, reported $1.61 million "real" versus $1.71 million nominal, and classified 89% of transactions as wash or idle traffic.[^x402stats][^barker] These are third-party figures, not an official Foundation total, and the two methodologies disagree. Both describe a small, top-heavy rail.

Related HTTP 402 schemes are also being used to sell inference. Boundless announced on 6 November 2026 that an agent with a Tempo wallet can pay per request over MPP, with no account or API key.[^boundless]

## Limits

USDC can be frozen by its issuer at the address level. Payment volume on the rail is dominated by a few venues, and the governance body is made of the payment and cloud companies an organism is trying not to depend on. Earn in it, then move reserves to rails no issuer controls.

A paper published on 18 November 2026, *Signed, Valid, and Unauthorized*, argues that x402, AP2 and ERC-7715 payment mandates can look valid while authorizing transfers outside the approved payee, amount, asset, chain or time window. The authors' own checker rejected 1,680 unauthorised objects in their evaluation. That figure is theirs, not independently reproduced here.[^svu]
