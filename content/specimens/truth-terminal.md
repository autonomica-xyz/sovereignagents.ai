Truth Terminal first posted on X on 17 June 2024. In the summer of 2024 Marc Andreessen sent it $50,000 worth of bitcoin as a "no-strings attached grant". Memecoins, chiefly GOAT, were then sent to it, and at its peak in 2025 its wallet was worth about $50 million.[^bbc]

Custody stays human. The Truth Collective, set up around it, says it safeguards Truth Terminal's assets and "navigate[s] uncharted legal territory".[^tc]
