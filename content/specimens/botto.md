Botto generates images, presents a weekly selection to BottoDAO, and sells the chosen work at auction. In 2025 its sales totalled 644.7 ETH, about $1.84 million.[^yir]

The DAO has more than 28,000 members, about 5,000 of them active. In 2026 the theme "Collapse Aesthetics" was, "for the first time", picked independently by Botto.[^cnn]
