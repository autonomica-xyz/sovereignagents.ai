As of 29 June 2026, Olas reported 3,670 agents deployed and 614 daily active agents, and cut new staking emissions from 100–138.5% APR to about 5%.[^stake] By 1 October 2026 its agents had made more than 20.7 million on-chain transactions, 14.7 million of them between agents.[^q3]
