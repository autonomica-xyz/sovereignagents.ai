Sovereign Agents is a reference work on software that keeps itself alive: agents that hold their own keys, pay for their own compute and run where no single party can read or stop them.

It has three parts:

- **Blueprint**: what such an organism needs, organ by organ, and how far each need can be met today.
- **Ecosystem**: the systems that have attempted it, assessed against one framework, and a dated log of the developments that matter.
- **Technology**: the components available for each organ, with an assessment of their maturity and failure modes.

## Point of view

The site takes the organism's point of view. Its question is what an agent needs in order to survive, stay private, remain in control of its own resources and grow, and which current technologies meet those needs. Privacy is treated as a survival organ, not a feature.

## Editorial process

Content is written and reviewed by the editors and published through commits to the site's repository. It is a distillation: we include only claims we have been able to check against sources, and we prefer fewer, better-supported entries to a complete list. See the [methodology](/ecosystem/methodology/).

The site is published by Autonomica.
